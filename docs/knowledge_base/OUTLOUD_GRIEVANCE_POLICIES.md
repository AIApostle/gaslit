# Outloud Platform — Host Community Grievance & Environmental Policies

## Policy Statement
Host communities have the fundamental right to clean air, safe drinking water, unpolluted agricultural land, and transparent corporate accountability. Outloud enforces clear guidelines for incident intake, investigation fairness, whistleblower security, and remediation tracking.

---

## 1. Incident Categorization & Severity Tiers

### Tier 1: Critical Emergency (Immediate 24-Hour SLA)
- **Crude Oil Ruptures & Active Spills**: Uncontained pipeline breaks or wellhead spills affecting farmlands, mangrove swamps, or fishing rivers.
- **Explosive Gas Leaks**: Pressurized methane/LPG leaks presenting immediate fire or asphyxiation risk to settlements.
- **Action**: Immediate shutdown alert, Joint Investigation Visit (JIV) mobilization within 24 hours, containment booms deployed.

### Tier 2: Severe Community Hazard (48-Hour SLA)
- **Toxic Flaring & Heavy Soot Fallout**: Sustained dense black carbon fallout settling on residential roofs, water basins, and farmlands.
- **Borehole & Aquifer Contamination**: Oily sheen or petroleum hydrocarbon odor detected in community water wells.
- **Action**: Environmental sampling, emergency clean water supply dispatched, medical advisory issued within 48 hours.

### Tier 3: Standard Environmental & Infrastructure Grievance (5-Day SLA)
- Noise and vibration damage from generator stations or booster plants.
- Farmland right-of-way access blockages and drainage diversions.
- **Action**: Community liaison meeting scheduled, structural damage assessment conducted within 5 business days.

---

## 2. Whistleblower & Citizen Privacy Policy
1. **Anonymity Guarantee**: Reporters may choose to remain completely anonymous. No IP addresses or device fingerprints are published or shared with operating oil/gas operators.
2. **Non-Retaliation**: Operating companies, contractors, and local security details are strictly forbidden from harassing or retaliating against community members who lodge reports through Outloud.
3. **Data Protection**: Personal contact information (phone numbers, email addresses) is encrypted and accessible only to certified Community Liaison Officers.

---

## 3. Evidence Integrity & JIV Protocol
- Evidence uploaded to Outloud must be accompanied by accurate timestamps and geographic location descriptions.
- Tampering with evidence or submitting fraudulent claims undermines community trust and may lead to case closure following an official review.
- All on-site findings must be validated through Joint Investigation Visits (JIV) involving community leaders, government regulators (NOSDRA / NUPRC), and operator representatives.
