# Outloud Host Community Grievance Platform — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. General Questions

### What is Outloud?
Outloud is an official conversational AI case management platform designed for host communities. It provides a direct, zero-paperwork channel to report environmental hazards (such as gas flaring, oil spills, and water pollution) and track case resolution with full accountability.

### How do I report a grievance?
You can speak or type in natural language with our AI Officer by clicking "Talk to AI Officer" or any of the category buttons on the portal. No forms or registration are required. Simply describe what happened, where it occurred, and when.

### Can I report anonymously?
Yes. You can report grievances without disclosing your real name. However, providing a phone number or email address helps our Community Liaison Officers contact you with critical safety updates and investigation outcomes.

### What types of incidents can I report?
You can report:
- **Gas Flaring & Soot Fallout**: Toxic emissions, continuous flaring, soot deposits on roofs/crops, excessive nighttime noise and vibration.
- **Crude Oil Spills & Ruptures**: Pipeline leaks, farmland damage, polluted fishing streams, wellhead leaks, and blocked farming access.
- **Water & Environmental Health**: Contaminated community boreholes, hydrocarbon odors in tap water, chemical spills, and respiratory health hazards.
- **Infrastructure & Community Safety**: Industrial road damage, heavy truck hazards, boundary disputes, or liaison officer misconduct.

## 2. Tickets & Tracking

### What is a Case Reference Code?
When your incident is recorded, Outloud automatically issues an official ticket code (e.g., `HCC-20260923-A1B2` or `ELEME-2026-001`). This is your permanent, tamper-proof system of record ID.

### What is the 6-Digit Verification PIN?
Along with your ticket code, you will receive a private 6-digit PIN. Keep this PIN safe. It is required to view confidential investigation notes and verify that you are the original reporter when checking status or speaking with officers.

### How do I check the live status of my case?
Open the chat widget and say: *"Check status for ticket HCC-20260923-A1B2"* (replace with your code). The assistant will verify your PIN and provide real-time updates on case stage, assigned officer, and recent actions.

## 3. Evidence & Investigation

### Can I submit photos or video evidence?
Yes. During or after your conversation with the AI Officer, you can upload on-site photos, videos, or documents directly into the chat. You can also attach additional evidence later by quoting your ticket reference.

### How long does an investigation take (SLAs)?
Cases are governed by strict Service Level Agreements (SLAs):
- **Critical / Tier 1 (Active Oil Spills & Severe Gas Leaks)**: Initial response and containment review within 24 hours.
- **Urgent / Tier 2 (Soot Fallout & Water Contamination)**: On-site verification within 48 hours.
- **Standard Community Grievance**: Investigation note and community briefing within 5 business days.

### What happens if my case SLA is breached?
If an assigned officer fails to update or resolve the case within the SLA window, the system automatically flags the case as `BREACHED` and escalates it to the Senior Community Operations Manager.

---

## 4. Human Officer Handoff

### How do I speak with a human Community Liaison Officer (CLO)?
If you need immediate human intervention, ask the AI Assistant: *"I want to speak with a human officer"* or click the **Escalate to Human** option. Your session and case summary will be routed directly to the on-duty Community Liaison Officer desk.
