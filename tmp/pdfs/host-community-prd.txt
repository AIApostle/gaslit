     1. Product Overview
     The Host Community Complaint & Case Management Platform is a case-management system for oil &

     gas operators and community-relations teams that need a reliable way to receive, track, investigate,

     resolve, and communicate updates about complaints raised by host-community members. The central

     problem is not simply collecting complaints; it is preventing complaints from disappearing into phone

     calls, letters, WhatsApp conversations, informal notes, or disconnected spreadsheets.

     A community member files a complaint—such as an environmental spill concern, compensation dispute,

     pipeline issue, access concern, or another community-related grievance. The platform converts that

     complaint into a structured case with a unique reference number, category, location, description,

     timestamps, responsible officer, current stage, SLA information, and communication history.

     The case moves through Reported → Under Investigation → Response Issued → Resolved, with

     Escalated available as a branch where an SLA is at risk or the case requires management intervention. The

     case-management application remains the source of truth for the case itself, while SwiftAgent's

     CRM/pipeline capabilities and Zapier automation handle contact synchronization, pipeline-stage updates,

     and automated status communications.

     In one line: A host-community grievance platform that turns a complaint into a traceable case, keeps

     staff accountable through workflow and SLA visibility, and keeps the complainant informed

     automatically as the case progresses.

     1.1 Product boundaries
•
      The app is the system of record for case-management data, workflow state, investigation notes, evidence,

      response records, and audit history.

•
      SwiftAgent is an integration/automation layer for contact and pipeline synchronization and automated

      status communications, subject to verification of the actual Zapier-exposed triggers and actions.

•
      Zapier is the workflow bridge between the case backend and SwiftAgent.

•
      The MVP does not claim automatic submission to a government grievance or regulatory system.

•
      Low-connectivity and low-literacy conditions are treated as product requirements rather than edge cases.

     The supplied template explicitly treated community-facing grievance reporting as a separate problem space; this PRD

 2. Problem Statement
 Host communities may raise complaints through multiple informal channels: phone calls, letters, visits to

 an office, SMS, WhatsApp messages, community meetings, or conversations with staff. These channels do

 not automatically create a single, accountable case record. After the initial complaint, the complainant can

 be left without a clear reference number, responsible officer, expected response timeline, or reliable way to

 know what is happening.

 This creates a trust and operational problem. A complaint may be known by one employee but invisible to

 management; a response may be drafted but not communicated; a case may remain unresolved beyond an

 internal SLA; and historical information may be difficult to reconstruct when management asks what

 happened.

 3. Solution
 The solution is a centralized complaint-to-resolution workflow. A complaint enters through a supported

 intake channel, is validated and converted into a structured case, assigned to a case officer, tracked through

 defined stages, and communicated back to the complainant whenever meaningful progress occurs.


 3.1 Intake
 A community member can submit what happened, where it happened, when it happened, and how they can

 be contacted. The interface should use plain language and avoid requiring the complainant to understand

 internal company terminology. Where connectivity and device limitations make a web form impractical,

 the product can accept the same core information through SMS or WhatsApp once the selected

 gateway/channel is implemented.


 3.2 Case creation
 The backend creates a structured case containing a unique case ID/reference, category, description,

 location, reported date/time, complainant/contact information, source channel, assigned officer, current

 stage, timestamps, SLA target, and communication history. The person is also created or updated as a

 contact in SwiftAgent and associated with the case through the integration.


 3.3 Pipeline tracking
 Every case follows a controlled lifecycle. The case officer changes the stage in the case-management

 application. The application records the change first; the integration then emits a webhook event. This

 preserves the principle that the app is the source of truth rather than allowing a CRM automation to

 become the authoritative case database.

 3.4 Automated communication
 On a valid stage transition, the backend sends a webhook to Zapier. Zapier performs the configured

 SwiftAgent action(s), such as updating the contact's pipeline stage and initiating the appropriate campaign

 or notification workflow. Exact SwiftAgent capabilities must be verified before production

 implementation.


 3.5 Internal management
 Case officers receive a working queue. Managers receive a higher-level view of case volume, ageing,

 unresolved cases, SLA risk, escalations and resolution patterns. This transforms grievance handling from

 an informal follow-up process into an observable operational workflow.

     4. Product Vision, Objectives & Principles
     4.1 Product vision
     To create a trusted, accessible and accountable digital channel through which host-community complaints

     become traceable cases, receive timely attention, and generate clear communication from report to

     resolution.


     4.2 Objectives
•
      Ensure every accepted complaint receives a unique reference and a persistent case record.

•
      Reduce the information gap between the complainant and the organization after a complaint is submitted.

•
      Give case officers a clear queue of work, ownership and next actions.

•
      Give managers real-time visibility into open cases, ageing, SLA risk and escalations.

•
      Automate repetitive status communications without removing human judgment from investigation or

      resolution.

•
      Maintain a reliable audit history of case progression and important actions.

•
      Support practical intake options for communities with varying connectivity, smartphone access and

     5. Market & Business Model
     The product is best positioned as a B2B SaaS case-management platform sold to organizations operating

     in or around host communities, particularly oil & gas operators, indigenous producers, energy companies,

     and organizations with dedicated community-relations or grievance-handling teams. It is not a consumer

     marketplace and is not a two-sided marketplace in the traditional sense.

 7. User Journey & Case Lifecycle
 The primary journey is designed to eliminate the gap between 'complaint received' and 'complaint visibly

 progressing.' The system should make each transition explicit and observable.

 1   Submit: The community member submits a complaint through the available intake channel.

 2   Validate: The system checks that minimum required information and contact details are present.

 3   Create: The backend creates the case, assigns a unique reference and stores the initial timestamp.

 4   Synchronize: The system creates or updates the complainant contact in SwiftAgent and records the

     integration relationship.

 5   Notify: A receipt/confirmation event is sent through the configured automation flow.

 6   Assign: A case officer is assigned according to the organization's workflow.

 7   Investigate: The officer gathers facts, records actions, attaches evidence and updates progress.

 8   Respond: The organization prepares and issues an appropriate response.

 9   Resolve: The case is marked resolved with a resolution summary and closure timestamp.

 10 Follow-up: The complainant can reference the case after closure, subject to retention and access rules.

 10. SwiftAgent Integration
 SwiftAgent is not the source of truth for the case. The case-management backend owns the case record and

 workflow state. SwiftAgent is used to extend that workflow with CRM contact management, pipeline

 representation and automated communication. Zapier acts as the event-driven bridge.


 10.1 Integration sequence
 1    The community member submits a complaint.

 2    The backend creates the case and assigns a unique reference.

 3    The backend creates or updates the complainant contact in SwiftAgent through the verified integration

      path.

 4    When a case officer changes the case stage, the backend creates a structured stage-change event.

 5    The backend sends the event to a Zapier webhook.

 6    Zapier maps the event to the appropriate SwiftAgent contact and pipeline stage.

 7    Zapier triggers the configured status communication/campaign for that stage.

 8    The backend records the integration event, result and timestamp for audit/monitoring.


 10.2 Example event payload
     { caseId, referenceNumber, contactId, category, stage, previousStage,
     timestamp, slaStatus }

 The exact payload should be finalized during implementation. Sensitive case details should not be sent to

 third-party automation systems unless necessary for the specific action and covered by the organization's

 privacy/data-processing requirements.

 13.4 Final case summary

 audit. It should include what was reported, where/when it occurred, actions taken, response issued,

 resolution, timestamps, responsible personnel and relevant communication history. Export is for human

 filing and internal use; it is not automatic submission to a government system.

 21. Acceptance Criteria
 The MVP is acceptable when the following end-to-end scenarios pass in a controlled test environment:

 1   A community member can submit a complete complaint and receive a unique reference.

 2   A case officer can find the new case in the dashboard and see its complete initial record.

 3   The officer can assign the case and move it from Reported to Under Investigation.

 4   The stage change creates an integration event without changing the authoritative case state outside the

     backend.

 5   The configured Zapier workflow receives the event and, where supported by the verified SwiftAgent

     integration, updates the contact/pipeline and initiates the expected notification.

 6   The case officer can record investigation activity and move the case to Response Issued.

 7   The complainant receives or has access to the corresponding status update through the configured

     channel.

 8   The case can be escalated and remains visible as an exception requiring management attention.

 9   The case can be moved to Resolved with a resolution summary and closure timestamp.

 10 All important case and stage events are visible in the audit timeline.

 11 If Zapier or SwiftAgent is unavailable, the case remains safely stored and the integration failure is
     visible and retryable.

 12 A manager can identify open, ageing, at-risk and escalated cases from the dashboard.

 13 A finalized case can be exported as a structured summary.

 14 Unauthorized users cannot access another complainant's private case information.

 21.1 Integration-specific acceptance gate
 No SwiftAgent integration claim should be marked 'passed' until the actual Zapier/SwiftAgent

 capabilities have been demonstrated in a test account. The prototype should document which actions

 are native, which require custom API/webhook steps, and which are not available.

 22. Conclusion
 The Host Community Complaint & Case Management Platform addresses a specific operational gap: a

 complaint should not disappear after a phone call, letter or message. Once accepted, it should become a

 traceable case with an owner, a defined lifecycle, a visible SLA, a complete history and understandable

 communication back to the person who raised it.

 The architecture deliberately separates responsibilities. The case-management application remains the

 source of truth. SwiftAgent contributes CRM/pipeline and automated communication capabilities. Zapier

 connects the two through event-driven workflows. This separation protects the integrity of the case record

 while still capturing the practical value of automation.

 The MVP should remain focused. Its success is not measured by how many features are added, but by

 whether a real complaint can reliably travel from Reported to Resolved while both staff and the

 complainant can see what is happening. The two major discovery gates—verification of

 SwiftAgent/Zapier capabilities and selection of an appropriate low-connectivity intake channel—should be

 resolved before the production integration is finalized.

 The product therefore provides a foundation for a more transparent and accountable host-community

 relations process, while leaving room for future expansion into multilingual intake, richer analytics, deeper

 CRM/HSE integration and additional community-engagement workflows.
